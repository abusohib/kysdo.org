About KYSDO

Who we are, how we are governed, and the approach that shapes every programme we run in Kaunia, Rangpur.

In this section
Overview Vision & mission Governance Institutional capacity Partnership Staff & structure

KYSDO is a non-profit, voluntary, non-political and non-discriminatory development organization committed to promoting sustainable and inclusive community development.

The organization works to empower youth, women, children, older persons, persons with disabilities and marginalized communities, with the goal of building a just, inclusive, environmentally sustainable and resilient society. KYSDO adopts a participatory, rights-based and people-centered approach to development.

Programmes span education, health, climate change adaptation, skills development, sustainable livelihoods, women's empowerment, social enterprise, disaster risk reduction and youth leadership — while promoting human rights, gender equality, social accountability and environmental sustainability.

Caption: KYSDO staff and volunteers at a community courtyard meeting, Kaunia.

Development approach

KYSDO adopts participatory planning, evidence-based decision-making, efficient use of local resources, gender-responsive programming, environmental sustainability, community ownership and partnership-driven implementation.

  • Meaningful community participation at every stage of the project cycle
  • Accountability to beneficiaries, donors and government stakeholders
  • Long-term sustainability designed into every intervention

“Sustainable development can only be achieved through active community participation, empowered youth leadership and effective partnerships.”

Md. Mozammel Haque · Chairman
