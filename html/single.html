Youth climate seminar in Kaunia brings 80+ participants together on adaptation and leadership

KYSDO convened youth volunteers, teachers, government officers and civil society at the Upazila Nirbahi Office hall to examine the role of young people in addressing climate change.

KYSDO Communications
Programme reporting team
Share Print

The seminar, titled “The Role of Youth in Addressing Climate Change — Challenges, Opportunities and Future Action,” was held on 05 July 2026 in the hall room of the Upazila Nirbahi Office, Kaunia, Rangpur. It was designed to raise awareness among young people about climate change and its impacts, and to open discussion on adaptation and mitigation strategies relevant to Bangladesh.

Participants included more than 50 youth volunteers alongside representatives from government offices, teachers, community leaders and civil society. Sessions gave space for research-based papers and knowledge exchange on climate-related issues affecting the district.

Topics of discussion

  • Climate change and the reality of Bangladesh
  • The role and responsibilities of youth in climate action
  • Innovative approaches to environmental conservation
  • Climate change and food security
  • The role of youth in disaster risk reduction
  • Climate justice and sustainable development
